The Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT Roorkee), located in Uttarakhand, is India's oldest engineering institution and a premier global center for scientific and technological education. Founded in 1847 during the British colonial era as the College of Civil Engineering, it was established to train officers and surveyors for the construction of the massive Ganges Canal.b Over the decades, the historic institution underwent several transformations—it was renamed the Thomason College of Civil Engineering in 1854, elevated to the University of Roorkee in 1949 as independent India's first engineering university, and finally rechristened as an "Institution of National Importance" under the IIT umbrella in 2001. Today, its beautiful 365-acre main campus blends majestic, colonial-era structures like the iconic James Thomason Building with state-of-the-art laboratories and research facilities.
Boasting over 20 academic departments covering engineering, sciences, management, and humanities, IIT Roorkee continues to be a trendsetter in path-breaking research and entrepreneurial innovation while producing an illustrious global network of nation-builders, scientists, and industry leaders.
